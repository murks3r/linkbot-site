# The redesign pass of 2026-10-24: decisions, one note each

Branch `vision/home-redesign`, from `build/vision` at `dd5d348`. Previews only; nothing merged until the owner approves.
The owner's brief: B as the hero, A's verdict sequence as the second section, C as its own page; a layer of dust instead of
the noise; the Venn as the signature object with two motion studies; two logo options; the brand lab's icon set; the type
pairing from the lab, self-hosted and subset; light and dark; every sentence true today.

## 1 · Texture: dust instead of noise

The paper texture (`public/material/paper.webp`, multiply over every page) is gone. In its place `Air.astro` draws one
fixed layer, `.dust`, from a 192 × 192 tile of grains (`public/material/dust-light.png` on light, `dust-dark.png` on dark;
one channel plus alpha, 30 KB each, generated once with a seeded random). The tile is drawn at one texture pixel per device
pixel (`--dust-size` 192 / 96 / 64 px by resolution), so a grain is never larger than a pixel; half the pixels carry a grain
at a random strength, the layer sits at `--dust-opacity` .055 (light) and .075 (dark), which comes to about one to two per
cent of darkening or lightening on average and never more than the layer's opacity on any pixel. It is alpha over
everything, header, text and buttons included, never a blend; static (no drift, so nothing to stop under reduced motion).
Text contrast: the darkest ink stays above 15:1, the secondary inks above 7:1, the blue above 6:1 in both schemes; the
browser acceptance measures the computed pairs in light and in dark (`SITE_SCHEME=dark`). The start values were tuned by
eye at 2× against the owner's words "only noticed when you look for it"; the first cut (.14 / .18) read as speckle.

## 2 · The Venn: 2D at the highest craft the page can carry, and why not 3D

`src/components/front/Venn.astro`: two discs of exact geometry (r 150, centres 160 apart at rest), each with a soft radial
shading, a lens that forms where they overlap and brightens with every criterion that meets, the nine verdict marks of one
synthetic pair settling into the lens, and the list beside it naming each criterion. Inline SVG, about four kilobytes, no
library, no WebGL.

Why (b) and not (a): a lit 3D volume with a glowing overlap says "the overlap is the match", a quantity; Linkbot's matching is
a verdict per criterion and no score, and the only honest thing to put in the lens is those verdicts, which need crisp marks
and words, not a shader. A raymarched pair would also cost a fallback image, a WebGL path, a lazy-loaded bundle and a
phone's battery for a picture that promises more than the product does. The 2D object loads with the page, prints, reads in
both schemes, and under reduced motion or without scripts the final frame simply stands.

Budgets: no layout shift (the figure's box is its viewBox); the page is usable before any script runs; 61–62 frames per
second measured in the recording at 1280 × 800; nothing is loaded beyond the page.

Two motion studies (the preview plays study 1; `?venn=2` plays study 2; both recorded in the evidence folder):
- **Study 1 "approach":** the discs start 400 apart and come together over 1.4 s (ease-out quint); the lens forms as they
  meet; then the nine marks settle into the lens one by one (160 ms apart, ease-out back), the lens brightening with each
  meets; rest at about 4 s.
- **Study 2 "meeting":** the discs rest overlapped with an empty lens; per criterion one small square leaves the need's far
  side and travels into the lens (420 ms, ease-in-out cubic), becomes the verdict's mark and settles into the grid; the lens
  brightens with each meets and dims a touch on conflicts; rest at about 5.5 s.
Polishing waits for the owner's pick.

## 5 · Type: the lab's D-P02, self-hosted, subset, with a scale

Chosen: **IBM Plex Sans SemiBold** for display and **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** for text, UI and numerals (Lane D,
exploration-r2-v1, pairing D-P02 "Belegte Klarheit"). Why: this site's content is evidence, criteria, units, codes, verdicts,
receipts; Atkinson is designed for character distinction (I, l, 1; 0 and O) and carries tabular numerals, Plex gives
headlines a rational, gridded form whose squared i-dot echoes the evidence square; the lab measured it as the strongest
profile and the smallest budget. Against: Plex carries an IBM association, Atkinson can read technical; the lab's warmer
alternative is D-P01 (Bricolage Grotesque + Hanken Grotesk).

Licences, read in the files: both SIL Open Font License 1.1. IBM Plex Sans declares the reserved font name "Plex", so its
subset may not carry that name: it ships as **"LB Display Sans"** (`public/fonts/lb-display-sans/`, with IBM's licence text as
`OFL.txt`). Atkinson Hyperlegible Next has no reserved name (`public/fonts/atkinson-hyperlegible-next/OFL.txt`). Web embedding
and subsetting are permitted by both; nothing is loaded from a third party.

Subsets (fontTools 4.60, WOFF2): Google's "latin" range plus ẞ, the sub- and superscripts and the arrows, which covers de,
en, es, it and fr. Files: LB Display Sans 600 (17.3 KB), Atkinson Next variable 200–800 (34.3 KB), and a 2.5 KB Plex Regular
face that fills the subscript digits (P₂O₅) and arrows Atkinson lacks. Total 54 KB, preloaded, `font-display: swap`, with
metric-matched fallbacks.

The scale (`src/styles/site.css`): display clamp(2.75–5.75 rem) / .98 / −.025 em (the home's hero a step smaller, 4.25 rem
max); h2 clamp(1.75–3 rem) / 1.06 / −.018 em; h3 clamp(1.2–1.4 rem) / 1.25 / −.008 em, 700; lede clamp(1.125–1.375 rem) /
1.5; body 1.0625 rem / 1.6; small .9375 rem / 1.5; caption .8125 rem / 1.4 / +.02 em; uppercase labels +.08 em, 700;
numerals tabular everywhere.

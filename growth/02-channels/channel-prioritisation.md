# Channel Prioritisation by Stage

Order of investment, not order of ambition. Each stage gates the next.

## Stage 0 — today (private beta, no traction, no analytics)

| Rank | Channel | Why first | Effort to start | Required assets | Conversion point | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Agency direct outreach** (C7) | Only channel that can produce revenue and pilot evidence without any audience; works on the agency's own pool | 4–6 h to prepare, 2–3 h/week | Reconciled prospect list; email/LinkedIn sequences; honest pilot scope; pricing stance (D1) | reply → 20-min call → pilot agreement | 20 qualified contacts without a single conversation → offline ICP/offer rework (do not scale) |
| 2 | **Blog engine** (C3) — drafts now, publish after review | Compounds; feeds every other channel; we already own verifiable material (benchmark, supply field report) | 3 h/week | 3 flagship drafts (below), architecture, review gate | article → try-the-search action; sources clicked | No qualified traffic by article #6 → topic/format rework gate |
| 3 | **Founder LinkedIn** (C1/C2) | Where DACH agencies and Talent actually are; network exists from day one (founder's own) | 2–3 h/week | profile hygiene; first 4 weeks of posts (drafted); commenting routine | profile→site visits; conversations; pilot asks | 2 posts/week for 6 weeks with no qualified sessions → format experiments before volume |

## Not prioritised (with reasons)

- **Paid ads** — no landing conversion evidence yet; would buy traffic into an unproven funnel. Revisit only with measured baseline conversions.
- **Mass content production** — explicitly banned by dispatch ("no mass thin SEO"); our constraint is verifiability, which scales slowly. Quality gate over volume.
- **Community building (own Discord/Slack)** — support burden outweighs benefit at this stage; participate in existing communities instead.
- **Podcast/conference tour** — no proof asset yet to talk about; revisit after pilot #1.
- **Affiliate/influencer deals** — no attribution infrastructure; risky claims environment.

## Balance rule

At any week in the 8-week plan: **at most 2 "push" channels** (high weekly effort: outreach, blogging) + **2 "pull" channels** (steady: LinkedIn, Reddit) + **1 "event" channel** (PH prep, newsletter). More than that and the cadence collapses — the plan's founder budget (≈7–9 h/week) assumes this balance.

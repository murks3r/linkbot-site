## Publication gates (repeat per item)

1. **Review gate** (owner): claims, proof labels, links/UTMs, images rights, names/permissions — see `02-channels/distribution-checklist.md`.
2. **Derivative gate**: every blog article ships with at least one LinkedIn derivative and one newsletter mention planned; derivatives may not overstate the article.
3. **Refresh gate** (quarterly): dated facts (BDU numbers, platform rules, benchmark numbers) re-checked; article footer updated; stale derivatives archived.

# LinkedIn Playbook — Founder-Led Publishing & Engagement

Status: DRAFT playbook — nothing is published from this file; post drafts live in `posts-weeks-1-4.md` and require owner sign-off before use.

Two surfaces, two jobs: **founder personal profile** (the engine: insight, build-in-public, agency conversation) and **company page** (the anchor: product facts, curated links, careers-adjacent updates). Posting happens from the founder's personal account; the company page reshares selectively. Company accounts have platform limitations for conversation features — a person is the voice. `[Provider claim: LinkedIn platform norms]`

## 1. Cadence (sustainable, ~2–3 h/week total)

| Day | Output |
|---|---|
| Tue | Main post (DE or EN depending on topic) |
| Thu | Second post (other language) |
| Fri | Comment/relationship block is part of daily 15 min (see runbook) |
| Daily | 15 min commenting — the real growth driver at this stage |

3 posts/week is the ceiling for one founder; 2 is acceptable in pilot weeks. Consistency > volume.

## 2. Formats (rotate; no single format dominates)

1. **Insight post** — one specific observation + one implication (text only, short lines).
2. **Evidence explainer** — "what we measure / what we won't claim", 3–5 short paragraphs (fits P2, P5).
3. **Build-in-public numbers** — our own repo-verifiable measurement, including the unflattering part (fits P4).
4. **Practitioner question** — one real question to a defined audience (sparks comments; use sparingly).
5. **Use-case walkthrough** — mandate→shortlist realism with synthetic/approved examples only (fits P6).

## 3. Hooks that are honest (use, reuse)

- "We measured X. The boring method won. Here's why we publish it anyway." 
- "The most useful word in recruiting software is 'unknown' — because it stops pretending."
- "A shortlist is a claim. Claims need sources."
- DE: "Wir haben unseren Matcher gegen Stichwortsuche getestet. Stichwortsuche hat gewonnen. Warum wir das trotzdem veröffentlichen."
- DE: "Eine Shortlist ist eine Behauptung. Behauptungen brauchen Quellen."

**Banned hooks:** "AI-powered", "10x", "we beat X", invented customer quotes, "stunning results", anything implying traction.

## 4. CTA grammar (soft, specific, one per post)

Approved CTAs: "Der volle Methodik-Artikel: [link]" · "If this problem sounds familiar, I'd like to hear how you solve it today." · "Try the guest search (no account): [link]" · "I'm looking for 2–3 agencies to test one mandate with this; DM open." — never "sign up now", never vote/share asks, never follow-bait.

## 5. Engagement workflow (manual, helpful, no tricks)

- **Who:** DACH recruiting/HR practitioners, job-search communities, recruiting-tech builders, first-party sources (BDU etc.), pilot agencies' people.
- **How:** answer questions in your actual area (evidence, privacy, matching methodology); add one specific fact or example; never drop a link unless it *directly answers* and policy allows (LinkedIn is a broadcast surface, so links in comments are acceptable when relevant — but sparingly).
- **No:** engagement pods, automated comments, auto-DMs, connection-request blasts, scraping tools, third-party schedulers that post for you. `[LinkedIn User Agreement §8 "Dos and Don'ts" — no bots/scraping/unsolicited mass invitations]`
- **Connection requests:** only with a real, sentence-level reason; no pitch in the note. Notes get 300 chars; use them for context, not for selling.

## 6. Measurement (qualified, not vanity)

| Signal | How | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified sessions | UTM'd links (`utm_source=linkedin`) → visit → guest search started | The only LinkedIn metric that matters at this stage |
| Conversations | Replies/DMs that pass "real question about their situation" | Pipeline for both flywheels |
| Follower quality | Manual sample monthly: are DACH recruiters + relevant Talent actually following? | Reach quality check |
| Post-level learning | After 20 posts: which topics/hooks produced conversations (qualitative log) | Feeds format rotation |

Never report likes/impressions as success. `[Hypothesis: founder-led posts will convert to conversations within 6 weeks; if not, formats are the variable to change first.]`

## 7. Language rules

- **DE** for agency topics (DACH), regulation, market numbers, practitioner questions.
- **EN** for Talent-side utility, methodology, engineering transparency.
- Numbers always with source + date ("BDU, April 2026"). Synthetic content always labelled ("fiktives Beispiel / synthetic example").
- If a post quotes a pilot outcome: written permission first; anonymize by default.

## 8. Company page policy (low frequency)

- Reshare founder posts that have product relevance; post 1–2/month: product facts, hiring-adjacent notes, curated industry reading.
- Same claim rules. No auto-feeds, no third-party scheduling of unattended posts.
- Job openings (when any): normal postings only; no "join our rocketship" copy.

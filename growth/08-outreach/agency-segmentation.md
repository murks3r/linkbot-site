# 20-Agency Segmentation Mapping (public data only)

Status: DRAFT template — the 20 rows are to be filled from the founder's existing prospect research (not accessible in this environment — see README dependency #1) combined with the public sourcing methods below. **No private data of any kind enters this file**: no candidate data, no scraped personal contacts, no purchased lists. Every row's `source` column must contain the public URL/source where the contact/company was found.

## 1. Why segment at all

The pilot offer (one mandate, evidence-labelled shortlist, no migration) fits some agencies and fails others. BDU's market data gives us the economic logic: the squeeze is hardest for firms **under ~1 m€ revenue**, while mid-size (1–7.5 m€) and Top-25 houses are more stable — and mid-size stayer firms are the ones motivated by *per-mandate efficiency* rather than platform transformation. Three of four firms expect platform-based/hybrid models to grow. `[Fact: BDU [1][2]]`

Working ICP hypothesis (to be falsified in discovery calls):
> A 2–15-person DACH recruiting boutique or mid-size house, retained or hybrid search in a discipline with hard verifiability requirements (engineering, data, regulated fields), whose shortlists clients actually question — and who cannot afford enterprise sourcing stacks. `[Hypothesis]`

## 2. Segment dimensions (public attributes only)

| Dimension | Values | Why it matters for the pilot |
|---|---|---|
| Size class (BDU classes) | boutique <250 k€ · small 250 k–1 m€ · mid 1–7.5 m€ · Top-25 >7.5 m€ | Effort tolerance, process maturity, budget speed |
| Discipline | engineering/data · finance · healthcare/life science · sales/marketing · generalist | Evidence requirements differ; our benchmark history is engineering/data |
| Engagement model | retained · contingency · hybrid | Retained = longlist scrutiny is the product; contingency = speed pressure |
| Regional footprint | DE metro clusters · AT · CH · remote-first | Where the founder can meet in person (real advantage in DACH) |
| Tooling posture (public) | ATS mentioned publicly? modern tooling content? | Signals openness vs. platform fatigue |
| Trigger signals (public) | growth posts, new desks, new niche launches, "we're hiring a researcher" | Timing: new desks feel shortlist pain first |

## 3. Fit score (score each row 0–2; max 12)

| Signal | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Size class | Top-25 | boutique | small–mid |
| Discipline evidence pressure | generalist | sales/marketing | engineering/data/regulated |
| Engagement model | pure contingency | hybrid | retained-dominant |
| Concrete shortlist pain visible (posts, job ads for researchers, public complaints) | none | implied | explicit |
| Reachable by founder (region/network/warm path) | cold & far | either | warm or local |
| Public tooling openness (talks about methods, not just clients) | closed | neutral | open/curious |

**Priority rule:** score ≥8 → approach in the first batch; 5–7 → second batch; <5 → hold. Re-score after every conversation (the score is a hypothesis about fit, not a diagnosis).

## 4. Public sourcing methods (where the rows come from)

1. **BDU member directory** — public search on bdu.de (Beraterdatenbank), filtered to Personalberatung; record only firm-level public info + the public listing URL. `[Fact: directory exists on bdu.de]`
2. **LinkedIn company search** — „Personalberatung"/"Executive Search" + region filters; use *public* company pages only; do not export personal data; contact routes via public contact channels.
3. **Industry event/association lists** — published speaker/participant listings (e.g., BDU events, regional recruiting meetups) — firm names as published, no attendee harvesting.
4. **Trade press** — who's quoted about shortlist quality/AI/regulation (Handelsblatt/Personalmagazin etc. coverage is a public signal, not a contact source).
5. **The founder's existing network** — warm paths (highest priority; log relationship type, not details).
6. **Existing research merge** — the launch research's 20 DACH prospects: *replace/merge rows here once available* (README dependency #1; do this before the first send, D5).

**Prohibited:** scraped contact databases · purchased lists · data brokers · LinkedIn export/automation tools · personal social profiles of employees beyond public professional pages · anything (anything) derived from candidate or ATS data.

# Newsletter Templates — "Linkbot Signal" (draft-only)

Status: **DRAFT — no list exists, no issue sent.** Signup mechanics must exist before any issue ships. The newsletter is earned attention: one issue/month maximum in the first months.

## Mechanics (before issue #1)

- **Double opt-in is mandatory** (DE practice and our own standard): a one-question signup (email + what you are: Talent / Agency / Curiosity), confirmation email, and an easy unsubscribe in every issue. No imported or bought lists, ever. Provider: pick one with EU data processing (e.g., EU-hosted options) — decision with owner, `[Owner task]`.
- **Sender:** Linkbot OÜ, identifiable physical note in footer; reply-to a real human address.
- **Segmentation:** one list is fine for the first months; if Talent vs Agency needs diverge, split then (not speculatively).
- **Metrics:** (1) replies (the honest metric at small scale), (2) click→landing (UTM `utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2026-MM-signal-N&utm_content=item-N`), (3) unsubscribe reasons when given. Opens are a proxy — don't optimize for them. `[Hypothesis: at n<200 subscribers, replies tell you more than any rate]`

## Structure (fixed skeleton — "one insight, one usefulness, one question")

1. **One insight** — something we measured or learned (with source/date; numbers honest, repo-verifiable where possible).
2. **One usefulness** — a practical item: a checklist, a template, a method.
3. **One question** — a real question to readers; replies shape the next issue.
4. **Product note (≤3 lines)** — what changed, what's next; no hype, no fake milestones.
5. Footer: unsubscribe, privacy link, why-you-got-this.

## Issue skeletons

### N1 — "The test pool flattered the baseline" (pre/at pilot stage; feeds from article a2)

- **Subject:** `Signal #1: Wir haben gegen Stichwortsuche verloren (und warum das gut ist)` — DE, since first contacts are DACH agencies; Talent sections in EN if list skews that way.
- **Insight:** the benchmark story in three sentences + link to a2. Include the honest loss prominently; that *is* the insight.
- **Usefulness:** "Vier Fragen an jedes Matching-Tool" (from a2's checklist) — printable one-page.
- **Question:** "Woran scheitert bei Ihnen eine Shortlist am häufigsten — Reihenfolge oder Begründung?"
- **Product note:** pilot model being scoped; first pilots opening; no price claims (D1 pending).

### N2 — "What the first pilot changed in our thinking" (anonymized; only after W5–6)

- **Subject:** `Signal #2: Was ein echtes Mandat mit unserer Review-Ebene gemacht hat`
- **Insight:** pilot learnings, anonymized and cleared in writing; numbers verified with the agency before sending; if nothing is cleared for reuse: write about method learnings only.
- **Usefulness:** "Der Ein-Mandat-Test" checklist (how to evaluate any tool in one mandate).
- **Question:** "Welche Evidenz überzeugt einen Kunden — und welche nicht?"
- **Product note:** second pilot status (without client names), one fix shipped from pilot feedback.

### N3 — "Month one, honestly" (W8)

- **Subject:** `Signal #3: Ein Monat, unsere Zahlen, unsere Lücken`
- **Insight:** our own funnel numbers (visits → searches started → first values), including the embarrassing parts; never other people's numbers.
- **Usefulness:** the public-supply field report's "four questions for job tooling".
- **Question:** "Was sollen wir als Nächstes öffentlich messen?"
- **Product note:** next 8 weeks' plan in three bullets.

## Writing rules

- Max ~350 words per issue; one CTA; no adjectives doing work that numbers should do.
- Every number carries source + date; every screenshot carries the synthetic-data label.
- Never: invented traction, "we're thrilled", countdowns, manufactured urgency, quoting the outdated price proposals.
- If there is nothing real to report: skip the month. Silence beats filler — the next issue then actually gets read.

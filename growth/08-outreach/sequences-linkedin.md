# LinkedIn Outreach Sequences (draft-only, manual)

Status: **DRAFT — not sent.** All rules from `04-linkedin/playbook.md` §5 apply: manual only, no automation tools, no connection blasts, no pitch in the connection note. This is *outreach*, separate from the content/engagement program — same account, stricter discipline.

## Sequence A — Agency principal (DACH, DE)

**Step 1 — Connection note (≤300 chars, no pitch):**
> Guten Tag {{first_name}}, ich beschäftige mich mit prüfbaren Shortlists (Evidenz statt Score) und tausche mich gern mit Personalberater:innen über Mandats-Realität aus. Freue mich über die Vernetzung — kein Verkaufsanliegen.

**Step 2 — After acceptance, +2 days (thank + context, still no ask):**
> Danke für die Vernetzung, {{first_name}}. Kurzer Kontext, damit Sie wissen, wer da in Ihrem Netzwerk sitzt: Ich baue an einer Evidenz-Ebene für Mandate — Brief → prüfbare Kriterien → Bewertung aus Ihrem eigenen Pool, jede Zeile begründet, Unbekanntes bleibt sichtbar. Wir suchen gerade 2–3 Agenturen für einen ehrlichen Ein-Mandat-Test. Wenn das für Sie kein Thema ist: völlig okay — dann kommentiere ich einfach gelegentlich Ihre Beiträge. 🙂

**Step 3 — +5 days (the actual ask, one time):**
> {{first_name}}, darf ich Ihnen eine konkrete Idee anbieten? 20 Minuten Videocall: Sie zeigen mir ein echtes Mandat (anonymisiert), ich zeige, was unser Ablauf daraus macht — inklusive der Stellen, wo er „unbekannt" sagt. Danach entscheiden Sie, ob ein Test überhaupt Sinn ergibt. Terminvorschläge: {{2 slots}}.

**Step 4 — close-out (if no reply after step 3, +7 days):**
> Alles gut, {{first_name}} — ich lasse es damit bewusst ruhen und wünsche Ihnen starke Mandate. Falls das Thema irgendwann relevant wird, melden Sie sich gern.

Rules: one ask maximum; if declined, no second attempt; if accepted and they don't book, one reminder max after 5 days; stop entirely on any "no".

## Sequence B — Agency principal (EN variant, 3 steps)

1. **Note:** > "Hi {{first_name}}, I work on evidence-based shortlisting (traceable review, no black-box scores). Connecting to learn from practitioners in the space — no sales pitch."
2. **After accept:** > "Thanks for connecting. Context: we're building the layer that turns a mandate into checkable criteria and reviews candidates from *your own pool* — every line sourced, unknowns kept visible. Looking for 2–3 agencies for an honest one-mandate test. If it's not for you, all good — I'll just be in your feed occasionally 🙂"
3. **Ask:** > "{{first_name}}, may I offer one concrete thing: a 20-min call where I run your (anonymized) mandate through our flow live — including the parts where it says 'unknown'. Then you decide whether a test makes sense. Slots: {{2 slots}}."

## Sequence C — Warm introduction (agency → agency, carried by the introducer)

When a pilot agency agrees to introduce a peer, *they* send it (`02-channels/referral-mechanism.md` template). We never "drop in" on the introduced contact without the introducer's message landing first.

## Conversation hygiene

- Log every step in the pipeline sheet (date, step, outcome) — LinkedIn has no lead memory; discipline lives in the sheet.
- Reply within 24 h during a running sequence; after close-out, no resurrection for 6 months.
- Never reference how many others we're talking to, never invent demand ("several agencies are waiting"), never quote the outdated price proposals as final.
- Screenshot only public profile content when logging context; no scraping tools, no export extensions.
- If a prospect asks "what does it cost": *"Scope and price are being finalized — first pilots get a fixed, written scope. I'll be transparent in the call."* (Until D1 is decided. Never present €149/€390 as validated.)

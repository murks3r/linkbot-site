# First-Customer Validation Sequence (agency pilot)

Status: DRAFT — plan for the owner; nothing sent, scheduled or priced. Two pricing proposals exist in older drafts (**€149 / 30-day pilot** in a product packet; **€390 / one-mandate "Evidence Sprint"** in a commercial packet) and **neither is validated** — they are treated throughout as competing hypotheses to be tested (D1), never as price lists.

Objective in one sentence: **get one real agency to run one real mandate through the review layer, produce evidence that the review is useful (or learn that it isn't), and convert (or decisively not) into a second mandate / paid continuation.**

## Stage 0 — Preflight (W1; all boxes before any contact)

- [ ] D1 pricing approach chosen ("discovery first" per recommendation); offer wording frozen with no price claim.
- [ ] Legal review of outreach messages completed (UWG §7 sensitivity; `08-outreach/sequences-email.md` compliance box).
- [ ] Prospect list reconciled to ≥20 rows with public sources (`08-outreach/agency-segmentation.md`, D5).
- [ ] Demo honesty state confirmed (Agent D's PR #1 fixes merged into the pitch we reference).
- [ ] Success criteria template written (below) so pilot agreements define "worked" *before* starting.
- [ ] Founder time blocked: 20-min calls × up to 10, plus pilot delivery hours.

## Stage 1 — Reachable-list calibration (W1–W2)

Rank the ≥20 rows: warm paths first (network, intro-capable), then high-fit cold (score ≥8). Public sourcing only. Target for outreach round 1: **10 contacted in two batches of 5** (quality of personalization over volume; E12 tests specificity).

## Stage 2 — First contact (W2–W4)

Sequence per `sequences-email.md` (4 steps) + `sequences-linkedin.md` as parallel-but-separate track for warm-ish contacts. Success gate for continuing: **≥2 qualified conversations booked from the first 10 contacts**; if not → rewrite angle, re-test 5 (never scale a failing message; failure modes: wrong list vs wrong offer — diagnose which via any replies received).

## Stage 3 — Discovery calls (W3–W5; 3–5 calls, 20–30 min)

**Call outline (scripted but human):**

1. Their world (5 min): niches, mandate flow, what happens between brief and shortlist today; who reviews; where it hurts. Listen for concrete friction (times, rework, client pushback).
2. The honest test question: "When you send a shortlist, how do you defend each line to the client — and what happens when a line can't be defended?" *(The answer is the entire business case.)*
3. Minimal disclosure of what we built (90 seconds): brief → criteria → evidence-labelled review **on their own pool**; no migration; nothing sent; UNKNOWN stays visible.
4. **Pricing probes (E3):** *"If we ran one mandate as a fixed, scoped test — what would feel fair for a one-off like that? What would make a higher number justified?"* Record verbatim. Never quote €149/€390 as our price.
5. Fit check both ways: their data-authority situation (can they authorize processing of their pool for this purpose?), timeline, who decides.
6. Close: propose the one-mandate pilot with written criteria as the next step, or note the reason for "not now".

**Disqualifiers (end politely, log why):** no real mandate within ~6 weeks · cannot authorize their pool for this purpose · expects candidate flow without doing review work · wants a black-box "send us candidates" service.

## Stage 4 — Pilot agreement (W4–W5)

One page, no legal fog: scope = one mandate, one shortlist cycle; their pool, their criteria, their decision; deliverable = evidence-labelled shortlist + a 30-min retro; fees per D1 decision (if charged: fixed, in writing); success criteria agreed from this menu (**their words override ours**):

| Criterion | How judged | Evidence source |
|---|---|---|
| Shortlist usable as-is or with light edit | recruiter verdict | retro notes |
| Every line defensible to their client | spot audit: pick 3 lines, ask them to justify from the output | output doc |
| Time saved vs their normal process (their estimate is the number) | their before/after estimate | retro notes |
| At least one **unknown** surfaced that they would otherwise have missed | count | output doc |

**Anti-criteria (we're honest in writing too):** not "better than other tools" (untested), not "candidates will be interested" (interest is not inferred, ever), not "fills the role" (delivery isn't ours to promise).

## Stage 5 — Delivery (W5–W6)

Run the mandate (`AGENCY_PILOT.md` workflow); measure what the dashboard needs (`09-measurement/dashboards.md` §2c); retro within 48 h; ask the referral question once if positive; begin case-permission flow (`08-outreach/case-study-templates.md`).

## Stage 6 — Conversion decision (W6–W7)

Offer path forward in their terms, in this order:
1. **Second mandate** (same terms) — the real product-market signal.
2. **Paid continuation** whatever D1 decided (only if pilot showed defensible value in their eyes).
3. **Free-again with case-study value** if both sides see learning value and their authority situation allows — with a hard stop at two.

## Stage 7 — Learning review (W7–W8) & kill criteria

Bring every outcome (including zeros) to the W8 review:
- **Kill criterion A (demand):** ≥20 qualified contacts + ≥5 qualified calls OR 20 contacts with replies — and still 0 pilots agreed → the offer doesn't land; reposition before any scaling.
- **Kill criterion B (value):** ≥2 delivered pilots, 0 second mandates and negative uses → the review layer isn't the bottleneck the market pays for; pause agency outbound, revisit ICP.
- **Kill criterion C (capacity):** delivery consumes >1 founder-day per mandate with no paid path → freeze pilots at 2 and solve delivery leverage first.
- **Continue criterion:** ≥1 second mandate or ≥1 paid conversion, with honest retro evidence → scale outreach carefully (batches stay ≤10/week).

## Pricing hypotheses (explicitly unproven — the required section)

| # | Hypothesis | Test | If supported | If falsified |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | A price point exists in the €100–500 per-one-mandate range that ≥30 % of qualified agencies accept for a scoped test | E3 probes across ≥10 qualified calls; first 3 written offers | Choose the frame with best accept/objection profile; D1 closes | Reposition: free foundational pilots + case-study value; price after value proof |
| P2 | €149/30-day and €390/one-mandate are *different products* to buyers (time vs deliverable), not two prices for one thing | Compare objections verbatim across frames | Standardize the frame buyers describe back to us | Merge into one "per mandate" unit |
| P3 | Agencies compare the price to recruiting-tool subscriptions (tens–hundreds €/month), not to placement fees (% of salary) | Objection analysis ("compared to what?") in calls | Position within tooling budget logic | Re-anchor toward fee logic (riskier; needs stronger value evidence) |
| P4 | First pilot cohort will pay *something* (any amount) rather than only free pilots | Count of written paid agreements among first 3 pilots | price ladder starts here | Free-cohort strategy with explicit time cap |

**Standing rules:** neither historical proposal is ever presented as validated · no price in writing until D1 · no discounting theater ("today only") · pilot fees (if any) are for work performed, refundable per written terms only.


## Jobs (a draft, planned, not built)

This section is part of the file only in builds that carry the draft jobs pages; a production build has neither.

- [Jobs](/jobs): a draft of the planned job board. Planned: postings from employers' own application systems whose terms
  allow reading them, each with its source, the day it was fetched and a link to the original posting; no posting
  text is kept, the original carries it. No job catalogue runs today: the postings on the board are made up in the shape the catalogue
  is planned to have. The board is a schema.org ItemList of the postings still open.
- Each open posting has a draft page at `/jobs/<id>`: its facts, the link to the original posting (which carries the
  text) and schema.org JobPosting with what the posting states, nothing added. A closing date is shown only when the
  source states one; a posting past it has no page and no place in the sitemap.
- Planned on top, not built: a covert yes for a person in work, with the same three steps. A check of each side; a
  private match, where a company sees the criteria and a verdict per criterion without the person's name, never a
  score; an introduction only when both say yes. A no on either side reveals nothing to the other.
- [Your own page in Jobs](/jobs/mine): a draft, in English and German, of what a person sees once signed in.
- The board, the posting pages and /jobs are in English, German, Spanish, Italian and French.
